Weekly Reports

PDF reports distributed via WhatsApp every Monday. Ask the clerk to trigger a send.

Check WhatsApp group
How to receive the WhatsApp Report
  1. A designated clerk sends "SEND REPORT" to the ministry WhatsApp group.
  2. The system detects the message and begins generating the PDF (approximately 30–60 seconds).
  3. The weekly PDF report is automatically sent back to the group.
  4. Monthly reports: clerk sends "SEND MONTHLY REPORT".
  5. Semester reports: clerk sends "SEND SEMESTER REPORT".
